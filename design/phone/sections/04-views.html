04 · The other views on a phone

Each view takes over the screen. Tap an object in the kitchen and its view fills the phone, with "‹ Kitchen" top left. The wide drawings are signed off; here each one becomes a single column. Nothing new is added.

Whiteboard

A to-do list that carries over. Nothing clears by itself. Tick a box to strike a line; the small red x wipes that one off. "Clear done" at the foot wipes every ticked line at once. It never clears unticked items. Drag the dots to reorder.

/whiteboard

After "Clear done"

Ticked lines gone. Brown butter cookies is wiped off and the lines below move up. The pill goes away until something else is ticked.

/whiteboard

Idea board

Notes tacked in one column. The cork scrolls up and down, newest first, with plain tacks and no statuses. The Archived envelope stays pinned top right. A paid-plan object, drawn for a developer.

/ideas

Recipe log

A ruled notebook page, as one scrolling column. Red margin, blue rules, dated entries newest first, the way the Trials notebook is written. Older entries scroll up from below.

/log

Cookbooks

One row of spines. A single row reads better: the spines keep their height and the stamped titles stay big enough to read. Two rows would halve each book. When the shelf fills, the row scrolls sideways.

/cookbooks

One cookbook, a page

Weekend Bakes, one page at a time. Croissants, page 11. The round arrows turn the page. Contents opens the first page of the cookbook; tapping a spine on the shelf opens Contents too.

/cookbooks/weekend-bakes

The contents page

The first page of the cookbook. You land here from a tap on a spine, or from the Contents button on any page. Recipes are in the developer's order, with drag dots and an x that takes a recipe out of this book only. A name opens its page.

/cookbooks/weekend-bakes

References

The shelf row turned upright. One column of cards, newest first, each with its kind stamp: Cookbook, Blog, Chef / Baker, Article, Tool. The two starters sit last, marked STARTER. References are free for everyone.

/references
