02 · The recipe box on a phone

The box, opened from the kitchen

/box/croissants

The whole screen is the box. No header and no bottom bar. The Croissants card rests on the ledge of the lid, the ☰ sits in the lid's corner, and the tabs run in two staggered rows: A, C, E in front, B, D behind, only for letters that have cards. The floating + is left off here (it shows while you browse tabs).

Tab C tapped: the fan

/box/croissants

Title-only cards rise above the tabs. They scroll sideways; tap one to put it in the lid, tap the lifted tab again to close. The lid is not dimmed, the open tab is lifted and the + is hidden. The address does not change.

The ☰ menu

/box/croissants

One menu, in sections: your name, Add recipe (type, URL, scan), View (Simple list on or off) and Sign out at the bottom.

Card View: the open question

  1. Question for Ari: Card View on a phone: A, any way you hold it (decision 003), or B, always a sideways card?

A · any way you hold it (Claude's pick)

/box/croissants

A · held sideways

/box/croissants

A is what decision 003 settled, and it does not fight the phone. Tap the card and Card View covers the screen however you hold it. Upright, the ingredients sit in a band pinned at the top that folds to one line, the steps scroll under it and one is marked "you are here". The sun keeps the screen awake. Sideways, ingredients are on the left and steps on the right, each scrolling on its own. The red X goes back to the box.

B · always a sideways card

/box/croissants

B turns the card, not the screen. The recipe opens as a landscape index card like the wide box's, drawn sideways on the upright phone, so you turn the phone to read it. The card in the lid stays a preview. There is no rotate prompt (decision 004).
