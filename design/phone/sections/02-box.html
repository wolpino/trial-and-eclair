02 · The recipe box on a phone

The box with a drawer

The drawer is the pick (Ari, 11/17). The lid card fills the screen, with ‹ › arrows at its edges for the previous and next card. A short strip of brown wood sits at the bottom with the brass "▲ A–Z" pull and a small round wooden + for a new recipe. This changes decision 003: the tabs are not always showing, and the + sits in the strip, not over the card. Swipe up or tap the pull to bring the tabs out; the tabs say what the box is, so there is no label.

Simple list

A plain list (003, PRD R6) that replaces the wood for screen readers and keyboard users: letter headings and recipe titles, nothing lost. The ☰ turns it off. It is a setting, so the address stays the same.

Card View

Card View follows the way you hold the phone, and the "⟲ Keep sideways" pill in the card's header row keeps it sideways when the phone is upright. The setting is remembered (Ari, 11/17; can be revisited). In every figure the step you are on is a yellow band across the whole step with a bold "▶ you are here". The sun keeps the screen awake; the red X goes back to the box.
