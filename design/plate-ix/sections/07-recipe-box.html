07 · The recipe box, opened

The open box, with a card on the ledge

Same box, opened. The cobalt box from the counter fills the screen. Its lid stands up behind it and the chosen card rests on a walnut ledge inside the lid. Ingredients are on the left, directions on the right, in handwriting on a ruled card.

Picking a card. Click a letter and its first card goes onto the ledge. The orange tab is the letter you are in; the glowing tab is the one being pointed at. "Next" and "Back" flip through the cards in A to Z order by title. The blank card at the very front is "Add a card": click it and a new blank card lands on the ledge, ready to type on.

The tabs. A to I in front, J to Q in the middle, R to Z at the back. R to Z stands straight behind A to I and J to Q sits in the gaps. Each row is a different paper so the rows read apart. Recipe cards show as white edges behind each divider.

Before a card is picked

The lid starts empty, at its smallest height (about 45% of the screen). A taped slip says what to do. Guests see the same box with the starter recipes in it and can add up to 5 cards of their own.

A long recipe

The lid stops growing at about 55% of the screen. After that the card scrolls inside the lid: the last line is cut off, a bar on the right shows where you are, and "More" scrolls down. This one is a starter recipe, so it has "Make it mine" (your own copy to change) where "Edit" would be.

Editing the card in place

Edit happens on the card itself. No form opens. Each line gets a thin underline and you type on it. A new line waits at the end of each list. "Add a photo" sits in the corner where the photo will be taped. Done saves, Cancel puts the card back as it was, and Delete is kept apart from both.

The card, full screen

For cooking from. The card alone, with large writing you can read from across the counter. Tap an ingredient or a step to tick it off; the step you are on is marked in yellow. Ticks clear when you close the card. "Back to the box" returns to the open box with this card still on the ledge. Close goes to the kitchen.

Turned over

The back of the card holds what you don't need while cooking: notes, where the recipe came from, and the photo. The corner fold and "Turn over" both flip it. Yellow words are links: here one goes to the notebook entry, one to an idea on the board.

Who gets what. Home cooks and developers both have the whole box. A guest gets the starter recipes and up to 5 cards of their own; after that "Add a card" asks them to sign up.
