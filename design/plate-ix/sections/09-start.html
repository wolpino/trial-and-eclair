09 · Getting started

Sign in

One recipe card for everyone. Sign in is its own page, outside the kitchen. The kitchen shows faintly behind so you know where you are going. The card reads like a recipe: what you need (username and password, the two fields the app has today), then the method, where the last step is the button. Two links sit at the foot: one to start a kitchen, one to try it as a guest.

Start a kitchen (sign up)

The same card, a little longer. It asks for what the app asks for today: username, email (optional), password, and the password again. There is no plan picker, because every new kitchen starts free, and the card says so in one line. Step 1 is the new question: start with sample recipes and notes, or start empty. "Yes" is picked for you. The yellow slip is clipped on only when you arrive as a guest. It tells you your cards come with you.

A brand-new kitchen with no samples

What "No, start empty" gives you. The six things you can open, each bare, with the one line it shows the first time you open it. Nothing is hidden: the kitchen looks the same, only empty. Switch the Plan to Home cook to see the paid three. They keep their "paid plan" tag and open to a sample you can look at but not change.

Removing the samples later

One go, from the Account menu. The line only shows while samples are still in the kitchen. One click opens the check on the right. It says how many items go and that your own things stay. After that the line leaves the menu.

  1. Is the recipe-card sign-in too cute? If so, I would keep the card and drop the "You'll need" and "Method" headings, leaving a plain form on ruled paper.
  2. Samples: is "Yes, show me around" the right default? The other way is to make people pick one before the button works.
  3. Username or email? The app signs in with a username today and email is optional. The card keeps that. Should the new design switch to email only?
  4. Where do guests land? I assumed the full kitchen with the samples in it, with the recipe box as the one thing they can add to (5 cards, kept 30 days). Or should "Try it as a guest" open the recipe box straight away?
