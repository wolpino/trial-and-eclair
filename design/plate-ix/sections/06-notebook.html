06 · The Trials notebook, opened

The notebook, open at the last trial you worked on

Same book as Plate VIII, with the two faults fixed. Nothing sits on the page number now, and the "+18 mm" note stands above its bar, clear of the taped photo. The paper holds only what the cook wrote. Every button is on one slim strip under the book.

Getting around. The folded corners turn the page: back to trial 3, or on to a blank page for trial 5. The paper tabs along the top are the recipes, in A to Z order, and the one in use is white. The orange ribbon marks where you left off, and the book opens there. "Contents" lists every trial. "Add a photo" shows how many of the five photos this session has used.

Contents

Every recipe with its trials. Each line has the date and the page. Click a line to open that trial. The yellow line with the ribbon is the one you worked on last. A small stamp shows which trial is published. "New recipe" at the end starts a new tab.

Compare trial 3 with trial 4

The older trial on the left page, the newer on the right. Lines that changed are marked in orange on both pages. Lines that stayed the same are left plain. "Done comparing" goes back to the normal spread.

What a home cook sees

A sample to read, not to write in. The pages turn, but nothing can be changed. The "paid plan" tag is always there, tied to the top of the book. The strip has one button, to see the plans.

  1. Tabs or contents? The main view has both recipe tabs and a Contents button. Keep both, or only one?
  2. Where does Publish live? It is a stamp on the strip under the book. Is that right, or should it sit only on the Contents page?
  3. Is Compare needed at first? It could wait until a recipe has two trials, or be left for later.
  4. Folded corners for turning pages: clear enough, or do you want plain arrows beside the book?
