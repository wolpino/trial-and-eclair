03 · The idea board

The idea board, opened

Open and type. Clicking the cork in the kitchen opens the board large. A blank note is already pinned at the top left with the cursor in it, so a new idea is just typing a title and pressing Enter. Each note is one idea: a title, sometimes one photo, and a tag.

Tack colour is the status. An orange tack means in progress. A blue tack means research. The small key pinned at the top right says so. Pointing at a note makes it glow and its tag turn yellow (shown on "Cardamom knots"). The notes sit in a tidy grid for now, newest first.

One idea, opened

The note comes forward. Clicking a note pulls it forward, still pinned, over the dimmed board. Everything on it can be changed in place: the title, the one photo, the tag, the status and a few lines of notes. Clicking the board behind puts the note back.

Three actions. "Start a trial from this" opens a new page in the Trials notebook with the idea's title, notes and photo. "Send to Next up" writes it on the whiteboard. "Delete" takes the note off the board.

An idea with no photo yet

A marked spot. A dashed spot the size of a photo says "Add a photo". An idea holds one photo only.

The new note, close up

Ready to add. The blank note starts as research (blue tack). Only the title is needed.

What a home cook or guest sees

Look, don't touch. The same board with sample ideas. There is no blank note and nothing can be changed. The first card says it is a sample and carries the "paid plan" tag. Notes still open to show what an idea holds, without the three actions.

  1. Grid or free placement first? This is a tidy grid, newest first. Is that enough for now, with dragging later?
  2. Is status by tack colour clear? Orange for in progress, blue for research, with the key in the corner. Or should each note also say the word?
  3. What should "Start a trial" carry over? Drawn as title, notes and photo. Does the idea then stay on the board, or come off it?
  4. Should the board have filters? For example by tag or by status. None are drawn. The find bar at the top could do this job instead.
