02 · The whiteboard

The whiteboard, opened

What it is for. "Next up" is the short list of what the cook is working on or will bake next. Click the whiteboard on the kitchen wall and it opens large, with the cursor already in the bottom line. Type and press Enter to add an item. A jot from the find bar can land here too.

Pinned or automatic. An item the cook put there holds an orange magnet. An item the app added has a grey "auto" ring and the reason next to it, such as "edited yesterday" or an open "next:" note. Click the dashed ring to pin an automatic item, so it stays. Click a magnet to take it off.

Using an item. The words are the link: pointing turns them yellow and underlined, and a click opens the recipe card, the Trials page or the idea named in blue underneath. Tick the square when it is done. Drag the dots on the left to change the order. Switch Plan to Home cook to see the same board with recipe cards only.

Ticking off

Three steps, top to bottom. Not done yet. Just ticked: a line goes through it. Later the line is wiped off and only a faint smudge is left.

Home cook

Same board, free plan. Every item opens a recipe card. Nothing is locked, so there is no paid label.

New kitchen

Empty. A light line says nothing is next up yet. The bottom line is still ready to type into.

  1. Removing automatic items: can the cook wipe off an item the app added, and if so should it stay away until something new happens on that recipe?
  2. After a tick: is a ticked item wiped off at once, or kept with a line through it until tomorrow?
  3. How many: the board holds 6 items. With more, should it scroll, or should the oldest automatic items drop off?
  4. The kitchen wall: the small whiteboard shows an orange dot on one item. Should that dot be the magnet (pinned), so the small and large boards agree?
