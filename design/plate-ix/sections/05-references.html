05 · References

The references, opened

Her research shelf. Clicking the bottom tier of the kitchen shelf opens this. It holds what she reads and uses, not her own recipes or cookbooks: cookbooks she owns, pastry chefs, blogs and tools. Each real reference is one object, and its shape and colour say what kind it is. The tape tabs under the shelf name the kinds and filter the shelf when clicked. A blank card lies on the desk, so she can add a reference by typing straight away. The same shelf is there on both plans.

One reference pulled out

The card. Clicking a spine pulls its card onto the desk and leaves a gap on the shelf. The card shows the title, the kind, the link, her notes, and the ideas and recipes it inspired. The yellow words are links: they open that recipe trial or idea. "Open link" opens the web address in a new tab.

The same card, being edited

Edit. Every part becomes a line to write on. The kind is one of four chips. "Link to an idea or recipe" finds one by name as she types. Delete asks once before it removes the reference. Link and notes can be left empty.

A new kitchen: nothing on the shelf yet

Empty. The shelf shows the blank spine, one line of help, and the same blank card on the desk.

  1. Are four kinds right? The app today has five: Cookbook, Blog, Chef / Baker, Article and Tool. I drew four and left Article out. Keep Article as a fifth kind, or fold it into Blog?
  2. Card first, or link first? I drew: a click on a spine pulls out the card, and "Open link" is on the card. Should a click go straight to the web page instead?
  3. Shelf order. I grouped the shelf by kind, with each tab under its group. Would you rather have newest first?
  4. Starter references. The app puts a few on every new shelf, and they can be hidden but not edited. Keep them, or start a new kitchen with the empty shelf drawn here?
