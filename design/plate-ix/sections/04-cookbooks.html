04 · The cookbooks, opened

The shelf: every cookbook, and a blank one to start

What opens first. Click the cookbook tier in the kitchen and the same shelf comes up large. Each binder is one cookbook. Its spine says the title, how many recipes are in it, and whether it is shared or still a draft. Click a spine to open that cookbook for reading. The blank binder at the end starts a new one.

Reading: the binder open on the counter

Left page. The cover page and contents in one: title, description, the share link with a Copy pill, then the numbered recipes. Each recipe name is a link that turns to that page. Right page. One recipe in full. The stamp says which day's copy this is. A cookbook keeps the recipe as it was when it was added, so later changes in the Lab do not show here. Getting around. The tabs on the right edge and the two arrows turn pages. The Edit pill at the top switches to editing.

Editing: same binder, same place

Left page becomes the worksheet. Title and description are boxes you type in. Each recipe is a strip: drag the dots to move it, the x takes it out of this cookbook only. Add a recipe clips a short list of your published recipes to the right page. Adding makes a copy as the recipe is today. Sharing sits under it: the link, Copy share link, and Unpublish (it reads Publish while the cookbook is a draft). Done goes back to reading.

What a home cook or a guest sees

Same binder, a sample inside. Nothing is hidden. The paid plan tag hangs from the name plate the whole time. There is no Edit pill and no share link. Pages still turn.

  1. Binder or bound book? I kept the ringed binder from the current app because pages that come out and move match "reorder and remove". Keep it, or draw a sewn book?
  2. Open to the shelf, or straight into the last cookbook? Drawn here: the shelf first. With only one cookbook, should it skip the shelf?
  3. Where does the share link live? Drawn twice: on the cover page when reading (link plus Copy) and in a Sharing card when editing (with Publish or Unpublish). Is one of those enough?
  4. Updating a recipe in a cookbook. Today the only way is take it out and add it again. Is that fine for now, or do you want a "Use the newest version" pill on each strip?
