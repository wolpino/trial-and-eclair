The recipe log cabinet: three cats, in turn

Cat A. A ginger tabby curled asleep in a cream mixing bowl, chin on a paw, tail wrapped round the front.

Cat B. A grey tabby loafed on a folded tea towel, paws tucked, looking out.

Cat C. A black and white cat curled asleep in a round bread proofing basket.

One cat each time. The cabinet rotates between these three. Each time it opens, one of them is inside, a different one from last time. Shown close up here; at full size they sit on the cabinet floor, as in the recipe log tile above.

Normal width and extra wide

Same scale, one above the other. At normal width (top) the left edge cuts off the fridge's hinges and the right edge cuts off part of the window and the sink. From a 1680 px browser (bottom) the kitchen extends: the hinges, the whole window and the whole sink show. Nothing in the middle moves.
