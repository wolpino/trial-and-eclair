More · Whiteboard

The whiteboard, opened. "Next up" is a short handwritten list of what the cook will bake next. Everything on it is written by the cook; the app adds nothing. The cursor is already in the bottom line: type, press Enter, and the line is added. Tick a box to put a line through an item; drag the dots on the left to reorder. The big view is in the ring above; these are its other states.

Ticking off

Ticked today. A marker line goes through the item and it stays on the board until the next day, so the cook can see what got done. A small red x sits just after its words, a little raised, and shows the whole time the item is ticked. Clicking the x wipes the item off at once and the items below move up. Here the pointer is on the x.

More than six items: the board scrolls

Long list. With more than six items the list scrolls inside the board. A marker arrow says how many more are below the frame, and a thin rail on the right shows where you are. The "write the next thing" line stays put at the bottom.

New kitchen: empty "Next up"

No words. Three marker doodles (a question mark, a little eclair, a whisk) are the only things on the board, with the write line ready below. They wipe off as soon as the first item goes on.
