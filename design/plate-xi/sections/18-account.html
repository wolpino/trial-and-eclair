More · Account and Settings

Change password

A plain form over Settings. Change password (and Change beside the username, which turns into an edit field with Save and Cancel) is opened from Settings. The password form is a small dialog over the dimmed sheet: current password, the new one twice, and one line on what a password needs. Cancel goes back; Save changes it and keeps her signed in.

Remove sample data: the confirm

Says plainly what goes and what stays. The button opens a small dialog over Settings. It lists what goes (the sample notebook recipes with their trials, the three sample ideas, Weekend Bakes, the sample box cards) and what stays: everything she made, and the starter references on her shelf. It cannot be undone, so the safe button, Keep them, comes first and the orange button names the action. Afterwards the Sample data part reads "Sample data removed" and the button is gone. A home cook's list is just the sample box cards.
