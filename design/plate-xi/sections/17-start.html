Where the guest link goes

Guests land in the kitchen. "Try it as a guest" sits at the foot of both cards, on the front page too. One click opens the whole kitchen with the sample data in it, not the recipe box on its own. From there a guest can add up to 5 cards to the recipe box (decision 002).

Seen signed out. Sign in, sign up and the front page come before anyone has a plan, so everyone sees the same cards. Each card is upright in the middle of the pale kitchen with only its hatched shadow. Its two printed headings are Ingredients (over the fields) and Method (over the button).
