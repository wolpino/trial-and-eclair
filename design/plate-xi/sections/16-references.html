More · References

The opened shelf is in the ring above. References are free: everyone gets this shelf, and a developer and a home cook see the same.

One reference pulled out

The card. Clicking a card pulls it onto the desk and leaves a gap on the shelf. It shows the title, the kind stamp, the date, the link, the cook's notes, and the ideas and recipes it inspired (yellow words open them). Edit and Delete are plain words by the title, the same as the recipe box. Delete asks once. "Open link" opens the web address in a new tab.

The same card, being edited

Edit. Every part becomes a line to write on. The kind is one of five chips. "Link to an idea or recipe" finds one by name while typing. Link and notes can be left empty.

After "Remove samples": the starters stay

Starter references. Every kitchen gets two starter references (here a pan size converter and an article on gluten), printed rather than handwritten and marked STARTER at the foot. Removing the sample data from Account takes away the sample references only; the starters stay, after anything the cook added. Pulled out, a starter shows a short printed About, an empty Notes and the link. A signed-in cook sees Edit and Delete by its title; editing a starter makes the cook's own copy and never changes the starter for anyone else.
