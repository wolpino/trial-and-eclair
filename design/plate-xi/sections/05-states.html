05 · Loading, error, offline, and the paid plan

Small states every view shares. Drawn once here, used by every open view. Day look only.

Loading

The view opens at once. The page is there straight away, with faint ruled lines where the words will be. Nothing spins and nothing jumps: the words fill in on the lines when they arrive.

Error

When a view cannot load. The page stays open and a small printed card is taped on it: "That didn't load." Try again asks once more. No codes, no technical words.

Offline

No connection. A thin printed strip sits just under the find bar and stays there until the connection is back. The kitchen shows what was saved on this device.

The "paid plan" tag, home cook

Click the tag and a card opens. It names what the Developer plan adds. The button switches the home cook to Developer at once. It is free for now, and nothing needs paying. "Not now" closes the card.

The "paid plan" tag, guest

A guest signs up first. The same card, but the button says "Sign up". Once signed up, the cook is offered the switch to Developer.
