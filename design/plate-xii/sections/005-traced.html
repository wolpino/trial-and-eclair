What changed this round

  • Bottles from your studies (B1 trio, B2 decanter, B3 five jars, B4 carafe and flask), redrawn in the kitchen's look: flat colour, the thick dark outline, a darker side and a white highlight, a soft tint where light comes through. The window stays walnut.
  • Crystals from your studies too: hexagonal prisms with a white top facet and a pale blue bottom, on the brass ring.
  • Rainbows follow the time of day instead of a sun patch: long and low across the wall in the morning, steep onto the counter at midday, up the wall in the afternoon, none in the evening, when the glass goes to dusk and the room dims a little.
  • Composition books in front of the whiteboard, now lying flat at the same angle as the plates. The photo is off the notebook page (tabs, ribbon and doodle stay).
  • Words: RECIPES on the box, "Baking log" at the top of the clipboard sheet, "Ideas" on the big note on the cork board.
  • Flour and rolling pin can come away; the wall and counter are filled in behind them.
