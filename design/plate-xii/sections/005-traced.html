What the script did

  • Sorted all 1,365 shapes into objects by where they sit, keeping the paint order, so each object can be lit as one.
  • Box: its shapes now use Blue 3 shades, so it stays the brand blue when the cabinets change. Polaroid backgrounds keep their own blue.
  • Recolour: the 8 traced cabinet blues became shades of one cabinet colour; the wall became one wall colour.
  • Cleared about 480 traced scribbles: the whiteboard, the notes, the notebook pages, the box tabs and the book titles. The app will write the real text.
  • Cropped the dark frame from the trace and rounded the numbers: 286 KB, about 90 KB as sent over the web (the plate drawing is about 30 KB).

What it can't do yet

  • Glow only, no movement. The ink lines are a few huge shapes shared by the whole kitchen, so one object can't lift without leaving its outline behind.
  • No fridge, no window seat for the spoon jar, no labels on the log drawers. These need drawing in the same style (in Kittl, or by hand on top).
  • One fixed picture. A wider screen scales it or shows plain wall at the sides; it can't reveal more kitchen. The phone needs its own trace (Kittl's closer crop would suit).
  • Evening would be a tinted layer on top, not a redrawn evening.
  • A few faint marks are left beside the mixer head; easy to clear by hand.
