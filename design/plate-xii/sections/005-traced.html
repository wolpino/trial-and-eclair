What changed this round

  • Pointing — no movement: C1 rise and C2 press are gone. Two new options alongside B3 sticker: G1 green a soft refracted light in the colour of the green glass bottle; G2 amber the same warmth as the morning room wash. B3 sticker now has the cream band flush tight to the ink outline (the gaps from v15 are fixed).
  • Rainbows — by time of day: all three kinds (beams, scattered flecks, spots on curved surfaces) are always on; what changes is the light itself. Morning and afternoon bring a dramatic warm golden wash from the crystal across the whole room; midday is crisper without the wash; evening has no rainbows. The R1/R2/R3 and look controls are gone.
  • Open cupboard interior: the jar outlines, lid edges, shelf boards, back-wall seams and door outline are now redrawn in the trace's thick wobbly ink style, to match the rest of the kitchen.
  • Window frame: the top, right and inner-left frame edges are overlaid with clean even strokes so the outline weight matches the other items.
  • Above the box lid: the wall just above the open lid is patched with wall colour, covering any stray marks.
