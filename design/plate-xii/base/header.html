The kitchen, straight on

Two redraws of the kitchen in the plate style, both seen straight on like Ari's Kittl desktop 2. A brings in as much of Kittl desktop 1 as fits: blue cabinets, tiled wall, the cork board with photos, the bracket shelf, the sash window with its sunbeam, Buttercup, the notebook lying open. B keeps desktop 2's arrangement and tidies it to the plan. Both are drawn as shapes, so every object is a button (point at one, or Tab to it), the cabinets, fridge, wall and backsplash take any colour, and nothing the app writes is baked in. Questions for Ari are at the top, each linked to its drawing.
