02 · Eclair marks

This reopens "crops of the eclair", on purpose. The eclair is redrawn here in the kitchen's style (the kitchen's own drawing is private to its file): choux body, chocolate glaze with drips, a white drizzle. G and H sit at the tape's angle (4 degrees, tilted up to the right). Each is shown big, at the top bar (28 px), 32 and 16 px, on paper and on Prussian. The small sizes are bolder, with no hatching.
