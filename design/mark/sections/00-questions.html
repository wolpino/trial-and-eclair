  1. B: white or blue tape? See the three B tapes. Letters: black on white, white on blue, or dandelion on blue. And at small sizes, padded or tight? Each is drawn both ways, and the padded tape is also shown big.
    Claude's pick: blue tape (cobalt) with white letters, padded at 32 and 16 px. White letters are the clearest on blue. Padding stops the letters touching the edge when small.
  2. Eclair: G plain, H with T&E, or I monogram? See G, H and I.
    Claude's pick: G, the plain eclair. H is too busy: at 28 px the letters turn to a smudge. I reads, but it is the most work for the least gain.
  3. Letters or eclair overall? Is T&E still the mark, is the eclair the mark, or both? See them in the app, including next to the title.
