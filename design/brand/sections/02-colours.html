Evening and the window

Evening is not a second palette. The day kitchen is drawn, then a warm wash falls from the top edge and is multiplied over it (dusk). Only the window sky changes colour. Open views always use the day look.

Lighter and darker shades

Shades are mixed from the colour, never picked by hand, so they follow a cook's own colour: light = 78% colour + white (cabinets 82%); dark = 78% colour + black; deep (cabinet shadow) = 55% + black; side (cabinet end) = 40% + black; box back = 72% + black.

Text on colour

Contrast worked out on this page (WCAG: 4.5 for body text, 3 for large text and drawn shapes). Orange is a fill only; orange words use the darker "orange text". When a cook picks their own colour, labels on it switch to ink or paper, whichever has more contrast.
