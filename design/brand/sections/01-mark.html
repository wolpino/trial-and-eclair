01 · The mark

Eclair G (picked 11/17): a plain eclair, tilted up to the right at the tape's angle (4 degrees). Choux in amber, a chocolate glaze with drips, a white drizzle, and the ink line on top. No letters on it.

Sizes

Two drawings. Full (above 40 px): hatched shadow, drizzle, four or five drips, 2.8 line. Small (40 px and below): no shadow, no drizzle, three drips, a thicker line so it holds. On the blue it gets a paper edge.

App icon and browser tab

A square cannot hold a wide eclair, so in the icon and the tab it turns to 35 degrees to fill the square. This tilt is for squares only; everywhere else it is 4 degrees.

With the title

On sign in and the front page: the eclair beside "Trial & Eclair" in Young Serif. The eclair is as tall as the capitals plus a little, and sits on the same line.

Rules

Do

Leave clear space round it of half its height. Keep the 4-degree tilt (35 in squares). Use the small drawing at 40 px and below.

Don't

Write on it, recolour it to match a cook's kitchen, flip it, straighten it, crop it, or put it on a busy picture.
