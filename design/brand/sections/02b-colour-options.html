A · The recipe box colour

Buttercup vs dandelion. Buttercup is the paler, softer yellow; dandelion (labels when pointed at, the main button, tags) is the bright one. A buttercup box would sit next to dandelion labels and the yellow note on the cork, so the box would read as one more yellow thing, not the centre.

B · Which colour leads (app icon, and the brand's one colour)

Prussian was never in the box. It was the valance (the scalloped band over the window in the 11/08 Kitchen Plate), and the Mark Plate kept it only as a dark ground. Nothing ties it to the kitchen now. The lead colour could instead come from the kitchen itself: the box (the heart of the app) or the cabinets (the biggest area).
