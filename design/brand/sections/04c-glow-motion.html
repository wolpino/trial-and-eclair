04c · Soft glow with movement (v3)

Ari likes movement but not a jump, and prefers the soft glow (P2). Four ways to combine them. Each drawing plays on a loop: resting, pointed at, resting. The movement is slow (0.35 s) and eased, and stops; nothing bounces. Day on the left, evening on the right.

Evening glow

The evening is a warm wash laid over the whole kitchen, and the glow sits under it, so the same halo comes out dimmer and browner at night. Two choices: keep the day halo (E1), or make the evening halo paler and stronger so it reads like lamplight (E2). Both with G1's slow rise.
