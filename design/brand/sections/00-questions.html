00 · Questions for Ari
  1. Recipe box colour: cobalt, denim, faded denim or buttercup?
    Claude's pick: denim (A2). Lighter and friendlier than cobalt, and still the clearest thing on the counter. Buttercup is lovely, but it is one more yellow beside the labels and the cork notes; it suits a cook's own pick better than the default.
  2. Which colour leads (the app icon and the brand's one colour)? Prussian, cobalt, denim, stem green, or sage white.
    Claude's pick: the box colour (denim, B3). The box is the heart of the app, so the icon is "the eclair on your recipe box". Green is the biggest area, but on a home screen green icons are common, and green with amber reads more like a sticker than a kitchen.
  3. What can a cook recolour? Cabinets only, box only, or everything with a wheel?
    Claude's pick: drop the wheel. Box only, from about five tested swatches ("your stand mixer colour", C2). One change a cook can feel, and nothing can look off. Cabinets could come later the same way.
  4. Pointing: P1 amber dots, P2 soft halo, P3 ink dots, P4 hatched halo, P5 lift, no glow, or P6 sun dots?
    Claude's pick: P5 with a little of P2: the object lifts and its label turns yellow, with a faint warm halo. Calm in a busy kitchen, and it still says "you can open this".
  5. "T&E" under the home screen icon only? See the icon and its label. Claude's pick: yes.
  6. Body font: keep the Apple-first stack? See the fonts. Claude's pick: keep it; check on Android in the test piece.
