04 · Line, colour, halftone, hatching

The printed cookbook plate: an ink line over flat colour, hatching for shadow, halftone dots for light. Draw back to front (back wall, contents, front panel).

RuleHow
Ink lineAlways ink (#1F1D1A), round ends and joins. 3 for an object's outline, 2.4 for bars and buttons, 2.2 for faces seen in depth, 2 for details, 1.4 for hatching and ruled lines.
ColourFlat, no gradients. Solid things: the colour sits exactly under the line, never off it. Only loose things (notes, petals, light) may drift 1 to 2 px.
HatchingShadows: ink lines at 45 degrees, 6 apart, 1.4 wide. A cast shadow is the shape's hatch, moved 5 right and 6 down, at 80%.
HalftoneLight and pointing: amber dots (r 2.7) on an 8 px grid turned 30 degrees, over pale dandelion at 45%. Sunlight uses pale cream dots on a 9 px grid. Light is off by default.
PointingGlow + label: the halftone glow spills about 45 px round the object and its label turns dandelion. Pressing drops it 2 px, like a button. Keyboard focus adds a dashed ink ring. No hover-only labels; every label is always there.
DepthOne-point perspective. Every edge going back runs to one vanishing point, x 900, y 230 in the kitchen's drawing (see section 06).
PaperThe page is printed paper: no plate border in the app; printer's marks are for the plates.
