02d · Kitchen colours (v3)

The original plan, as Ari chose: cooks change the cabinets, fridge, wall and backsplash. Each offers five swatches chosen to sit with the walnut, the ink line and the blue box, plus a custom colour wheel at the end. The box stays the brand blue. Try it: click the swatches and the kitchen redraws.

The custom wheel (the rainbow swatch) opens a colour picker. Two guards keep it from looking off: labels on the chosen colour switch to ink or paper automatically, and shades (door panels, shadows) are mixed from the chosen colour so the drawing still holds together.
