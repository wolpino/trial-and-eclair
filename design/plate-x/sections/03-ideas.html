03 · The idea board

The idea board, opened

Open and type. Clicking the cork in the kitchen opens the board large. A blank note is already pinned at the top left with the cursor in it: type a few words, press Enter, and it is pinned. Newest ideas come first.

Short notes, plain tacks. Each idea is a short note: a title and a line or two. The notes sit in a loose grid, each a little crooked. The tacks are just tacks in the kitchen colours, mixed; they mean nothing. No statuses and no filters. Pointing at a note makes it glow (shown on "Cardamom knots").

More than fit. The cork shows as many notes as fit. The last row is cut off by the frame, a slip says how many more there are, and a thin rail on the right shows where you are, like the whiteboard.

Plans. Switch the Plan to Home cook or Guest: the same board with sample ideas, read-only. The blank note's place holds a sample card with the yellow "paid plan" tag and a "See the paid plan" link (a guest sees "Sign up"). Notes still open, without the buttons. The figures below are the Developer board.

One idea, opened

The note comes forward. Clicking a note pulls it forward, still pinned, over the dimmed board. The title and the few lines can be changed in place. Clicking the board behind puts it back. "Start a trial" opens the Trials notebook at a new trial, with "from the idea: Miso caramel" on its first page (see the notebook section). "Send to Next up" writes it on the whiteboard. "Delete" takes it off for good.

Before: Start a trial on Miso caramel

After: back on the board, the gap stays

The idea leaves the board, quietly. Once the trial starts, the Miso caramel note is archived. Back on the board its place is empty and the other notes stay put, so nothing jumps around under the cook's eye. The board tidies up (closes the gap) the next time it is opened, or when a new idea is pinned. The Archived envelope goes from 4 to 5. No "Moved to Trials" trace is left behind: the notebook opening at the new trial already says what happened, and a trace would be one more thing to clear off the cork.

The Archived envelope

A quiet way back. A small envelope pinned in the top right corner holds the archived ideas. Clicking it slides out a card: each idea, the day it left, and a link to the trial it became. It stays small so the board is about new ideas.
