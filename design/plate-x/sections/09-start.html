09 · Sign in and sign up

Three card styles for sign in

A · Recipe card

B · Plain index card

C · Tucked in the recipe box

The same form on three papers. A is ruled and laid out like a recipe card in the box: an orange rule under the title, blue rules, and printed headings ("You'll need", "Method") in place of Ingredients and Directions. B is a plain white index card with one strip of tape and nothing else on it. C stands in the blue recipe box, behind the letter tabs, as if it were the first card in it. All three say "Trial & Eclair" at the top. This is the only place in the app where the name is a title.

Sign in, in style A

Its own page, outside the kitchen. The kitchen shows faintly behind. Two fields, the two the app has now: username and password. Every word is printed; the only handwriting would be what a cook writes, and there is none here. At the foot: "New here? Sign up" and "Just looking? Try it as a guest". "Start a kitchen" is gone everywhere; the words are "Sign up" and "Sign in".

Sign up, in style A

What the app asks for now. Username, password and the password again. No email. Sample recipes are on by default. The box is ticked: the new kitchen opens with a few sample recipes, ideas and notes to look around. The line under it says they can be removed later from Account, all in one go. Untick it to start empty. One button: Sign up.

Where the guest link goes

Guests land in the kitchen. "Try it as a guest" sits at the foot of the sign-in card, under "Sign up". One click opens the whole kitchen with the sample data in it, not the recipe box on its own. From there a guest can add up to 5 cards to the recipe box (decision 002).

No Plan switch versions. These pages are seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, so Developer, Home cook and Guest all see the same drawings.
