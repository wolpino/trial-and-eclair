The two printed headings on the card. Ari is still choosing. The first sits over the fields (username and password), the second over the button. A is the classic recipe-card pair and is used in the drawings below.

Sign in

Its own page, over the pale kitchen. The lined recipe card sits upright in the middle, with only its hatched shadow: no tape and nothing in front of it. It has the same place and size as the front page card (section 12), so going from the front page to sign in only swaps the card. "Trial & Eclair" is the title; the front page and these two cards are the only places the name is a title. Two fields, as the app has now: username and password. All the words are printed. At the foot: "New here? Sign up" and "Just looking? Try it as a guest". The words are "Sign up" and "Sign in"; "Start a kitchen" is gone.

Sign up

What the app asks for now. Username, password and the password again. No email. Sample recipes are on by default. The box is ticked, so the new kitchen opens with a few sample recipes, ideas and notes. The line under it says they can be removed later from Account, all in one go. Untick it to start empty. "Try it as a guest" is here too, under "Sign in".

Where the guest link goes

Guests land in the kitchen. "Try it as a guest" sits at the foot of both cards. One click opens the whole kitchen with the sample data in it, not the recipe box on its own. From there a guest can add up to 5 cards to the recipe box (decision 002).

No Plan switch versions. These pages are seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, so Developer, Home cook and Guest all see the same drawings.
