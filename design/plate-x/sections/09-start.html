09 · Sign in and sign up

Where the card rests: four ways

A · On the open notebook

B · Tucked into the notebook's cover

C · Leaning on the recipe box

D · On the counter, between the two

One card, four places. The card is always the lined recipe card from the box: an orange rule under the title and blue rules below. It is never taped and never floats; it rests on or against something real. A and B look down on the counter. In A the card lies on the Trials notebook, open at a page. In B it is tucked under the notebook's front cover, its corner sticking out. C and D are seen from the front, like the kitchen. In C the card stands on one corner and leans on the closed recipe box. In D it stands upright on the counter against the wall, with the notebook on its stand on the left and the recipe box on the right. The notebook and box are the kitchen's own drawings, made larger. D is used below: the card reads straight on, and both objects frame it.

C · You'll need, Method

The two printed headings on the card. The first sits over the fields (username and password), the second over the button. A is the classic recipe-card pair and is used in the drawings below.

Sign in

Its own page, over the pale kitchen. The card stands on the counter between the notebook and the recipe box. "Trial & Eclair" is the title; this is the only place in the app where the name is a title. Two fields, as the app has now: username and password. All the words are printed. At the foot: "New here? Sign up" and "Just looking? Try it as a guest". The words are "Sign up" and "Sign in"; "Start a kitchen" is gone.

Sign up

What the app asks for now. Username, password and the password again. No email. Sample recipes are on by default. The box is ticked, so the new kitchen opens with a few sample recipes, ideas and notes. The line under it says they can be removed later from Account, all in one go. Untick it to start empty. "Try it as a guest" is here too, under "Sign in".

Where the guest link goes

Guests land in the kitchen. "Try it as a guest" sits at the foot of both cards. One click opens the whole kitchen with the sample data in it, not the recipe box on its own. From there a guest can add up to 5 cards to the recipe box (decision 002).

No Plan switch versions. These pages are seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, so Developer, Home cook and Guest all see the same drawings.
