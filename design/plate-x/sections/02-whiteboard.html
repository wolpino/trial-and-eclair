02 · The whiteboard

The whiteboard, opened

What it is for. "Next up" is a short handwritten list of what the cook will bake next. Everything on it is written by the cook; the app adds nothing. Click the small whiteboard on the kitchen wall and it opens large, with the cursor already in the bottom line. Type and press Enter to add a line. The first three lines match the kitchen wall: Croissants · trial 4, Rough puff pastry, Rhubarb galette.

Using it. Tick the box to put a line through an item. Drag the dots on the left to reorder. When a line names one of the cook's recipes, a small blue "recipe card" link sits under it and opens that card. Developer and Home cook get the same board.

Ticking off

Ticked today. A marker line goes through the item and it stays on the board until the next day, so the cook can see what got done. Pointing at the tick again shows "Remove now".

Tick clicked again. The item is wiped off at once, without waiting for tomorrow. The items below move up.

More than six items: the board scrolls

Long list. With more than six items the list scrolls inside the board. A marker arrow says how many more are below the frame, and a thin rail on the right shows where you are. The "write the next thing" line stays put at the bottom.

New kitchen: empty "Next up"

No words. One big marker question mark is the only thing on the board, with the write line ready below it. It is wiped off as soon as the first item goes on.

Guest

Where a guest differs. The board holds starter items from the starter recipes (Chocolate chip cookies, Sunday pancakes, Banana bread). Ticking and reordering work, but nothing is saved: the board is back to the starter items next time. The write line is replaced by "Sign up to write your own list".
