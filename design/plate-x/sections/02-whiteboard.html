02 · The whiteboard

The whiteboard, opened

What it is for. "Next up" is the short list of what the cook will bake next. Click the small whiteboard on the kitchen wall and it opens large, with the cursor already in the bottom line. Type and press Enter to add an item. The first three items match the kitchen wall: Croissants · trial 4, Rough puff pastry, Rhubarb galette.

Pinned or automatic. An item the cook wrote has an orange marker star beside it: it stays until the cook ticks it. An item the app added from recent activity has a grey "auto" ring and the reason next to it. Each automatic item has a little eraser on the right to wipe it off, and a dashed ring to pin it.

Plans. The whiteboard is free, so Developer and Home cook get the same board. The only difference is where automatic items come from: for a developer, trials and recipes; for a home cook, recipe cards only (switch Plan to Home cook). A guest's board is different and is drawn further down.

Wiping off an automatic item

Before. Pointing at the eraser beside "Rough puff pastry" shows a yellow "Wipe off" label.

After. The item is gone and a faint smudge is left. It stays away until something new happens on that recipe, such as an edit or a new trial. Then the app may write it back.

Ticking off

Ticked today. A marker line goes through the item and it stays on the board until the next day, so the cook can see what got done. Pointing at the tick again shows "Wipe off now".

Tick clicked again. The item is wiped off at once, without waiting for tomorrow. The items below move up.

More than six items: the board scrolls

Long list. With more than six items the list scrolls inside the board. The bottom item is cut off by the frame, a marker arrow says how many more there are, and a thin rail on the right shows where you are. The "write the next thing" line stays put at the bottom.

New kitchen: empty "Next up"

No words. One big marker question mark is the only thing on the board, with the write line ready below it. It is wiped off as soon as the first item goes on.

The small whiteboard's star

Close-up of the kitchen wall. A pinned item on the small board gets the same orange marker star as on the big board, so neither looks like a round cork-board tack.

Guest

Where a guest differs. The board holds starter items from the starter recipes (Chocolate chip cookies, Sunday pancakes, Banana bread). Ticking, wiping and reordering work, but nothing is saved: the board is back to the starter items next time. The write line is replaced by "Sign up to write your own list".
