The notebook, open at Croissants trial 4

The book is back to full width. Same composition-book spread as Plate IX. The tabs and arrows sit outside it, over the dimmed kitchen.

Tabs are this recipe's trials only. Croissants has four trials, so there are four tabs, plus Contents at the top. Tabs for pages before you stick out on the left, the rest on the right. Colours: left tabs custard (--custard), right tabs light stem green (--cab-hi), the selected tab orange (--orange), the same orange as the recipe box's letter tab you are in. Here trial 4 is the last trial, so every tab is on the left; the Contents view below shows right-hand tabs.

Two ways to move. The folded corners turn to the previous or next trial of this recipe ("trial 3" on the left). The round arrows outside the book go to the previous or next recipe, A to Z: Brown butter cookies and Rhubarb galette.

Publish (option A drawn here). A dashed "PUBLISH" stamp sits at the top of the trial's first page, beside the title, like an approval stamp on a lab report. Click it and the trial goes to the recipe box as a card. Both options are compared below.
