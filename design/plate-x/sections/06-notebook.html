06 · The Trials notebook, opened

The notebook, open at Croissants trial 4

No black band on top. The cover no longer peeks out above the pages, so the top of the spread is just the page edges.

Tabs are numbers. Contents, then this recipe's trials 1 to 4. The page title already says "Trial 4: croissants", so the tabs carry no name. Left tabs custard, right tabs light green, the selected tab orange.

Moving through the book. The folded corners turn to the previous or next trial of this recipe; their words are written in the page margin. The plain round arrows beside the book go to the previous or next recipe, A to Z, the same arrows as the recipe box, with no labels. Contents and the find bar reach any recipe directly. Page numbers sit at the centre bottom of each page.

The strip. New trial, Add to recipe box and New recipe on the left, Add a photo on the right. "Add to recipe box" opens the recipe box with a new Croissants card filled in from this trial, open for editing. The card's title is the recipe name; the cook can change it there. The page itself stays clean.

Plans. Switch the Plan to Home cook or Guest for the read-only notebook: same sample book, tabs, corners and arrows still work, but no writing buttons, and the yellow "paid plan" tag on the top of the book. Guest is the same drawing as Home cook; only the button reads "Sign up" instead of "See the paid plan".

Contents, at the front of the book

Every recipe and its trials. Click a line to open that trial. The tabs belong to the recipe you worked on last, Croissants, marked "last worked on" in the list. Contents is selected (orange) on the left; trials 1 to 4 are on the right in green. A small inked "IN THE BOX" mark shows which trial is on the card that went into the recipe box.

Adding a recipe that already has a card

First time, no question. Clicking "Add to recipe box" on trial 4 goes straight to the recipe box with the new card filled in (the recipe box section draws it).

Again, from trial 5. Croissants already has a card from trial 4, and one recipe has one card, so clicking the button asks over the dimmed notebook: replace the Croissants card, save trial 5 as a new recipe, or cancel.

Then the card opens. Either choice opens the card in the recipe box, ready to edit.

Past the last trial

Turning the corner on the last trial. Trial 4 is Croissants' last trial and has no card yet, so the next page is blank and asks, quietly, "Start trial 5?". Nothing happens unless the cook clicks.

A trial started from an idea

From the idea board. Moving "Miso caramel" to a trial archives the idea (it leaves the board) and starts a new recipe here. Its first page says where it came from, in small writing under the title.
