06 · The Trials notebook, opened

The notebook, open at Croissants trial 4

No black band on top. The cover no longer peeks out above the pages, so the top of the spread is just the page edges.

Tabs are numbers. Contents, then this recipe's trials 1 to 4. The page title already says "Trial 4: croissants", so the tabs carry no name. Left tabs custard, right tabs light green, the selected tab orange.

Two ways to move. The folded corners turn to the previous or next trial of this recipe; their words are written in the page margin. The round arrows go to the previous or next recipe, A to Z, each with a small paper tag tied on: Brown butter cookies and Rhubarb galette. Page numbers sit at the centre bottom of each page.

Add to recipe box (stamp spot A, the lean). A dashed "ADD TO RECIPE BOX" stamp sits at the top of the trial's first page, beside the title. Click it and the recipe box opens with a new Croissants card filled in from this trial, open for editing. The card's title is the recipe name; the cook can change it there.

Plans. Switch the Plan to Home cook or Guest for the read-only notebook: same sample book, tabs, corners and arrows still work, but no stamp or writing buttons, and the yellow "paid plan" tag on the top of the book. Guest is the same drawing as Home cook; only the button reads "Sign up" instead of "See the paid plan".

Contents, at the front of the book

Every recipe and its trials. Click a line to open that trial. The tabs belong to the recipe you worked on last, Croissants, marked "last worked on" in the list. Contents is selected (orange) on the left; trials 1 to 4 are on the right in green. A small "IN THE BOX" stamp marks the trial that went into the recipe box.

Where "Add to recipe box" lives: two ways

A · Stamp beside the title (the lean)

Before and after. Pointed at, the dashed stamp glows. Once added, it is inked "IN THE RECIPE BOX" with the date, and clicking it opens the card in the recipe box. Drawn in every figure above and below.

B · A button on the strip

Before and after. "Add to recipe box" sits next to New trial. Afterwards it reads "In the recipe box 11/11/26" and opens the card. The page itself stays clean.

The stamp's wording: three ways

A · Add to recipe box

B · Make recipe card

C · Into the recipe box

Same stamp, three words. A is drawn everywhere else on this plate. The button in option B would use the same words.

Adding a recipe that already has a card

First time, no question. Clicking the stamp on trial 4 goes straight to the recipe box with the new card filled in (the recipe box section draws it).

Again, from trial 5. Croissants already has a card from trial 4, and one recipe has one card, so a slip asks over the dimmed notebook: replace the Croissants card, save trial 5 as a new recipe, or cancel.

Then the card opens. Either choice opens the card in the recipe box, ready to edit.

Past the last trial

Turning the corner on the last trial. Trial 4 is Croissants' last trial and has no card yet, so the next page is blank and asks, quietly, "Start trial 5?". Nothing happens unless the cook clicks. The arrows still go on to the next recipe.

A trial started from an idea

From the idea board. Moving "Miso caramel" to a trial archives the idea (it leaves the board) and starts a new recipe here. Its first page says where it came from, in small writing under the title.
