12 · Front page and not found

The front page (A, picked)

What someone sees at the site root before signing in. One card over the still kitchen. The kitchen behind is the sample kitchen a guest would land in, pale and not clickable, with no find bar and no Account. The name is the title. "Trial & Eclair" heads the card, set the same way as on sign in; the front page and sign in are the two places it appears as a title. Under it, one line on what the app is, then three short lines for the three main things.

Three ways in. Sign up is the yellow button, because most people here are new. Sign in sits next to it. "Try it as a guest" is a link under them; it opens the kitchen with the samples in it, and a guest can add up to 5 recipe cards (decision 002). It is the same card on the same pale kitchen as sign in (section 09), so clicking Sign in or Sign up only swaps the card. No tape. The card floats over the kitchen like an open view, with only its hatched shadow; it will rest on the same thing the sign-in card picks (the notebook and/or the recipe box, being drawn in section 09), so the two match.

Not found, signed out: its own page

A small, friendly dead end. A blank recipe card lies on a bread board with a pencil, and a printed line says the page is not here. One button goes back. Signed out, there is no kitchen to show, so it is a plain page with the small name at the top left, and the button reads "Go to the front page".

Not found, signed in: over the dimmed kitchen (maybe)

The same card and words, opened like any view. Signed in, the not-found page could open over the dimmed kitchen with the find bar and Close from the frame, the same as every other view. The button reads "Back to the kitchen", and Close does the same thing. Compare it with the plain page above.

No Plan switch versions. The front page is seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, and the not-found page is the same for every plan. Developer, Home cook and Guest all see these same drawings.
