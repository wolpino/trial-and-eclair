12 · Front page and not found

The front page (A, picked)

What someone sees at the site root before signing in. One card over the still kitchen. The kitchen behind is the sample kitchen a guest would land in, pale and not clickable, with no find bar and no Account. The name is the title. "Trial & Eclair" heads the card, set the same way as on sign in; the front page and sign in are the two places it appears as a title. Under it, one line on what the app is, then three short lines for the three main things.

Three ways in. Sign up is the yellow button, because most people here are new. Sign in sits next to it. "Try it as a guest" is a link under them; it opens the kitchen with the samples in it, and a guest can add up to 5 recipe cards (decision 002). It is the same card on the same pale kitchen as sign in (section 09), so clicking Sign in or Sign up only swaps the card. No tape. The card floats over the kitchen like an open view, with only its hatched shadow; centred and upright with nothing in front of it, the same as the sign-in card, so the two match.

Not found, A: an index card over the kitchen

A plain index card. It opens like any view: centred and upright over the dimmed kitchen, with the find bar and Close from the frame. No tape and nothing under it. The words are typeset: "Page not found", "This page is not here.", one short line, and "Back to the kitchen". Close does the same as the button.

Not found, B: a cookbook with a page ripped out

The cookbook, opened at a missing page. The same sewn book as the cookbooks view (section 04): typeset pages, cloth spine, the thread in the gutter. The right-hand page has been ripped out, leaving a torn stub at the gutter and the blank page under it. The message and "Back to the kitchen" are set on the facing page.

Signed out. The same drawing sits over the pale kitchen (as on the front page) with "Go to the front page" in place of "Back to the kitchen"; no separate drawing is needed.

No Plan switch versions. The front page is seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, and the not-found page is the same for every plan. Developer, Home cook and Guest all see these same drawings.
