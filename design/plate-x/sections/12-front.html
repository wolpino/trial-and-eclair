12 · Front page and not found

Three ways to draw the front page

A · The kitchen behind a card

B · The kitchen, with labels

C · A plain printed page

What someone sees at the site root before signing in. A: the kitchen stands still and pale behind one card that says what the app is and gives the three ways in. B: the kitchen in full colour, nothing dimmed; printed labels point at the notebook, the shelf, the board and the recipe box, and pointing at any of them glows as usual. Clicking an object opens it as a guest. C: no kitchen behind, just a printed page with a small drawing of the counter, like a plate in a cookbook.

The name stays small. "Trial & Eclair" is a title only on sign in, so here it is a small printed name at the top left, where the find bar would be on the right. The kitchen itself is the picture.

The leading one: A, large

One card over the still kitchen. The kitchen behind is the sample kitchen a guest would land in, pale and not clickable, with no find bar and no Account. The card says what the app is in one line, then three short lines for the three main things. Three ways in. Sign up is the yellow button, because most people here are new. Sign in sits next to it. "Try it as a guest" is a link under them; it opens the kitchen with the samples in it, and a guest can add up to 5 recipe cards (decision 002).

Why A leads. It is the same card on the same pale kitchen as sign in (section 09). Clicking Sign in or Sign up only swaps the card, so the page does not jump. The kitchen behind it says "this is a kitchen" without asking anyone to read.

Not found: an empty recipe card

A small, friendly dead end. A blank recipe card lies on a bread board with a pencil, and a printed line says the page is not here. One button goes back. Nothing else on the page, so there is nothing else to get lost in.

Another way: a page torn out of the notebook

The same words in the notebook. The left page holds the message; a few torn stubs at the gutter show where a page was. Drawn here as someone signed out sees it.

Signed in or signed out. The way back is the only thing that changes. Signed in, the button reads "Back to the kitchen". Signed out, it reads "Go to the front page". Either drawing works with either button.

No Plan switch versions. The front page is seen signed out, before anyone has a plan, and the not-found page is the same for every plan. Developer, Home cook and Guest all see these same drawings.
