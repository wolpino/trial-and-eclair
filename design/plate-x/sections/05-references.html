05 · References

The references, opened

Her research shelf. Clicking the lower tier of the kitchen shelf (under Cookbooks) opens it large over the dimmed kitchen. It holds what she reads and uses, not her own recipes: a cookbook she owns, a pastry chef, a blog, an article, a tool. Each reference is a card standing on the shelf, newest first from the left, with the date it was added at the foot.

Five kind stamps. Each card carries a printed stamp at the top, one shape and colour per kind: Cookbook (blue, square corners), Blog (amber, round ends), Chef / Baker (orange, cut corners), Article (cinnamon, double line) and Tool (green, notched ends). The title on the card is in her hand, because she wrote it. A blank card lies on the desk, so she can add a reference by typing straight away.

One reference pulled out

The card. Clicking a card pulls it onto the desk and leaves a gap on the shelf. It shows the title, the stamp, the date, the link, her notes, and the ideas and recipes it inspired (yellow words open them). Edit and Delete are plain words by the title, the same as the recipe box. Delete asks once. "Open link" opens the web address in a new tab.

The same card, being edited

Edit. Every part becomes a line to write on. The kind is one of five chips, Article included. "Link to an idea or recipe" finds one by name as she types. Link and notes can be left empty.

After "Remove samples": the starters stay

Starter references. Every kitchen gets two starter references (here a pan size converter and an article on gluten). They are printed, not handwritten, and marked STARTER at the foot. Removing the sample data from Account takes away the sample references only; the starters stay, after anything she added herself. A new kitchen that signed up without samples looks the same. A starter has no Edit; a signed-in cook can hide one from her own shelf.

Who can do what. References are free. A home cook gets exactly the shelf drawn here, the same as a developer, so there is no home cook drawing and no paid tag. A guest is the same too: she can add, edit and delete her own references. The one difference: a guest cannot hide a starter reference (decision 002: guests can't remove starters).
