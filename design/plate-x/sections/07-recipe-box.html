07 · The recipe box, opened

The open box, with a card on the ledge

What changed. The box body now runs past the bottom of the screen, so it reads as a deep box and not a short tray. The lid stands up behind it and the card rests on the walnut ledge inside the lid. A photo is taped to the card front, top right. There is no back of the card: where the recipe came from sits under the title, and notes have a small box at the bottom.

Moving through cards. The arrows left and right of the card, inside the lid, go back and next through the whole box, A to Z by title. The tag under the next arrow says where you are: "B · 10 of 15". The square icon above it opens the card full screen.

Edit and Delete are a plain pencil and bin right after the title. Tabs are all one paper; only the letter you are in is orange.

Same for everyone. The box is free, so Developer and Home cook see exactly this. Switch the Plan to Guest to see the guest slip; the guest figures further down show what else differs.

Clicking a letter tab

A list first. Click a letter and the lid card lists that letter's titles. Up to 12 fit in two columns. More than that and the two columns scroll together: here B has 15, the bottom row is cut off and the bar on the right shows there is more. Pick one (Blondies is being pointed at) and its card goes onto the ledge.

Next runs on through the box

The last B card. The tag reads "B · 15 of 15" and "C next": one more click on the next arrow opens the first C card. Back runs the other way, so from the first B card it goes to the last A card.

Adding a card

A blank card at the back. It stands behind Z, taller than the tabs, with a "+ ADD A CARD" tape along its top. Shown as it looks when pointed at. Click it and it lands on the ledge, ready to write on.

The card, full screen

For cooking from. The card covers the whole screen: no kitchen, no find bar. Large writing you can read from across the counter. The source sits next to the title, the photo on the right and the notes box at the bottom. The red X, top right, goes back to the open box with this card still on the ledge.

Ticks. Tap an ingredient or a step to tick it off. Here three ingredients and two steps are ticked, and the step you are on is marked in yellow. Ticks clear when the card closes.

Notes. A small handwritten box at the bottom of the card, on the ledge and full screen. It holds up to 280 characters and scrolls inside itself when the note is longer than the box. While editing, a small "212 / 280" counter shows in its corner; it is hidden the rest of the time.

Guest

Where a guest differs. The box holds the starter recipes, each with a STARTER tape. Starter cards can't be edited or deleted and have no notes, so the bottom of the card holds "Make it mine" (your own copy, which uses a slot). A label stuck on the box front says how many of the 5 guest cards are used.

Guest, at the limit

One look for guest limits. Anything about the guest limit is a label: cream paper with a dusty rose border and a dusty rose "GUEST LIMIT" band in ink. That is the label on the box, and this card. Dusty rose (#e3a9ae) is used for nothing else, so it always means "guest limits". With all 5 used, the blank card at the back puts this on the ledge instead of a new card. Deleting one of their cards frees a slot.
