07 · The recipe box, opened

The open box, with a card on the ledge

What changed. The box body now runs past the bottom of the screen, so it reads as a deep box and not a short tray. The lid stands up behind it and the card rests on the walnut ledge inside the lid. A photo is taped to the card front, top right.

Moving through cards. The arrows left and right of the card, inside the lid, go back and next in A to Z order by title. "B · 2 of 4" under the next arrow says where you are. The square icon above it opens the card full screen.

Same for everyone. The box is free, so Developer and Home cook see exactly this. Switch the Plan to Guest to see the guest slip; the guest figure further down shows what else differs.

Clicking a letter tab

A short list first. Click a letter and the lid card shows that letter's titles. Pick one (Blondies is being pointed at) and its card goes onto the ledge. From there the arrows step through B, then on into C.

Edit and Delete, two ways

A. By the title. A pencil and a bin right after the recipe name. Easy to find, close to what they change.

B. Bottom right corner. The same two icons tucked in the corner, out of the way of the title and the photo.

Adding a card, two ways

A. A blank card at the back. A blank card stands behind Z, taller than the tabs, with "+ Add a card" written along its top edge. Shown here as it looks when pointed at. Click it and it lands on the ledge, ready to write on.

B. A button in the lid. An "Add a card" button under the back arrow, inside the lid. Same result: a new blank card on the ledge.

The card, full screen

For cooking from. The card covers the whole screen: no kitchen, no find bar. Large writing you can read from across the counter, and the photo stays on the front. The red X, top right, goes back to the open box with this card still on the ledge.

Ticks. Tap an ingredient or a step to tick it off. Here three ingredients and two steps are ticked, and the step you are on is marked in yellow. Ticks clear when the card closes.

Guest

Where a guest differs. The box holds the starter recipes, each with a STARTER tape. Starter cards can't be edited or deleted, so the pencil and bin become "Make it mine" (your own copy, which uses a slot). A slip taped to the box says how many of the 5 guest cards are used. At 5, Add a card asks them to sign up instead. Everything else is the same as the home cook.
