01 · The kitchen

The main kitchen, by day

What changed. The fridge is cream and the Recently card is held by two magnets. The window is lower and larger, with the sink in front of it. There is no sun patch: by day the light is just the window. The shelf labels are tape written in marker capitals. The whiteboard now says "Croissants · trial 4", to match the notebook.

Try it. Click the window and a tiny bird flies past. It also flies by on its own about every 20 seconds (if your computer is set to reduce motion, only on a click). Click the eclair to take a bite. The Plan switch shows the paid tags for a home cook and a guest, and a guest also gets a small "Guest" tag beside Account.

The evening kitchen

Evening. The same kitchen with a dark sky, a moon and stars in the window and a warm wash from the top. A shooting star crosses the window now and then, and when you click it.

How the Recently list is held

A. Magnets. A clean index card with straight edges, a red rule under the title and blue rules below. Two round magnets hold it.

B. Ticket rail. A steel rail with one small ticket per recipe and a tape label above.

Every name is a link. Croissants is shown as it looks when you point at it.

The floor, four ways

A. Black and white tiles. Every tile edge runs to the one vanishing point, so the edges no longer read as stripes.

B. Plain boards. Wood boards running away from you.

C. One flat colour. A pale terracotta.

D. No floor. The cabinets run down to a plain base.

The sink, two ways

A. Set into the counter. A steel sink in the counter top, with a sink cabinet below: two doors, no drawers. The main kitchen uses this one.

B. On the counter. A white enamel basin and the tap stand on the counter. The cabinets below stay as they were.

Normal width and extra wide

Same scale, one above the other. At normal width (top) the left edge cuts off the fridge's hinges and the right edge cuts off part of the window and the sink. From a 1680 px browser (bottom) the kitchen simply extends: the hinges, the whole window and the whole sink show. Nothing in the middle moves.
