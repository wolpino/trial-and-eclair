The main kitchen, by day

What changed since your notes. The board is a little narrower and the fridge and window sit a little further out, so there is more wall between things (fix A below). The sink has a false drawer front, and the other cabinets get a matching drawer line. Pinned items on the small whiteboard have an orange marker star.

Try it. Click the window and a tiny bird flies past. In this mock it also comes on its own every 20 seconds so you can see it; the app would use a slower timing (to be decided, about every 5 minutes). The evening kitchen

Evening. The same kitchen with a dark sky, a moon and stars in the window and a warm wash from the top. A shooting star crosses the window now and then (every 20 seconds in this mock), and when you click it.

Wood boards in the whole kitchen

Boards. Plain wood boards running away from you, every seam toward the vanishing point. The main kitchen keeps the black and white tiles.

The false drawer front under the sink

Under the sink only. The sink cabinet's doors start lower than their neighbours, so its door tops step down and the run looks uneven.

A matching drawer line (main kitchen). Every cabinet gets a drawer at the top, so the false front lines up with real drawers.

Does it look odd? On its own, a little: one cabinet with a different top makes the run read as mismatched pieces. With the drawer line along the whole run it reads as one fitted kitchen, which is what real kitchens do, so I suggest the drawer line.

Calming the cabinets, three ideas

A. Quieter. A darker, softer green with thinner lines, so the shelf and books lead.

B. Flat slab doors. No set-in panels, just a door and a knob.

C. Deeper shadow. A deep hatched shadow under the counter's front edge pushes the cabinets back.

The main kitchen keeps the current cabinets until you pick. A and B can also be combined.

Recently on the fridge

Settled: magnets. A clean index card held by two magnets. Each name is a link; Croissants is shown as it looks when you point at it.

Normal width and extra wide

Same scale, one above the other. At normal width (top) the left edge cuts off the fridge's hinges and the right edge cuts off part of the window and the sink. From a 1680 px browser (bottom) the kitchen simply extends: the hinges, the whole window and the whole sink show. Nothing in the middle moves.
