01 · The kitchen

The main kitchen, by day

What changed since your notes. The cabinets keep the original green, so the eye goes to the recipe log. Its RECIPE LOG tape label is now on the left door of the double cabinet, and the COOKBOOKS label has moved left along its shelf edge (REFERENCES stays at the right). The smaller board and the black and white floor are settled. Under the counter, left to right: the fridge, a slim cabinet (now the narrowest), the bookshelf (moved left), the double cabinet for the recipe log (with its pulls at the two inner top corners), and the sink cabinet with its false drawer front. A drawer line runs along the whole run. Brass bar pulls: level on drawers, upright on doors, always on the flat frame of the door.

The evening kitchen

Evening. The same kitchen with a dark sky, a moon and stars in the window and a warm wash from the top. A shooting star crosses the window now and then (every 20 seconds in this mock), and when you click it.

The recipe log cabinet

Open. The RECIPE LOG tape label sits on the left door, and the cabinet glows when you point at it. Clicking opens both doors. The log is a clipboard hung on a screw on the inside of the left door; its clip holds a sheet headed "Recipe log" with one row per bake: the date, the recipe and a short note, such as a substitution. The clipboard is only a glimpse: clicking it opens the recipe log full size, the way every object opens into its tool, so its small size on the door is fine. The cabinet is no longer empty: an animal lives on its floor. Shown here, Cat A, a cat asleep in a mixing bowl; the other animals are below.

Hinges. The doors have concealed hinges, fixed inside the cabinet, so from the front they do not show. Their hinge cups show on the backs of the open doors here.

Normal width and extra wide
