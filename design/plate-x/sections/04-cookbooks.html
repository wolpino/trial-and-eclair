04 · The cookbooks, opened

Step 1: the spines, close up

What the Cookbooks tier opens. The shelf comes up large with the books standing on it. Each is a sewn book with a rounded cloth spine and two raised bands; the title is stamped up the cloth, and the count of recipes sits small at the foot. From above you can see the page block and the sewn gatherings behind the headband. Click a spine to open that book. New cookbook, under the shelf, adds a blank book to the end of the row.

Step 2: the book, at its cover

The cover first. A spine opens the book closed, cover up: cloth spine, printed paper board, the title and a small printed plate. Share sits on the cover. Click the cover or the round arrow to open it at the contents.

The book open: contents and a recipe

Left page: contents. The five recipes in the book's order, with the date each was added and its page. Each name turns to its page. The x takes a recipe out of this book only.

Right page: one recipe in full. The thread of the sewing shows in the gutter, and the folded gatherings show at the head of the spine. Turning pages. The plain round arrows beside the book, the same as the notebook and recipe box. The strip. Add a recipe and New cookbook.

Plans. Switch the Plan to Home cook: the same three steps, with one sample book, Weekend Bakes, read only. No Share, no x, no strip buttons, and the yellow "paid plan" tag on the book. The strip offers "See the paid plan". Guest is the same drawing as Home cook; only that button reads "Sign up".
